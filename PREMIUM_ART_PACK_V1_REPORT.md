# Pachangas IQ Premium Art Pack V1 Report

## Scope

This phase explores 20–30 reviewable premium proposals without granting ownership, changing reward mappings or activating future product domains. Assets remain laboratory-only until a later product decision.

## Existing baseline

- `TeamShieldView` already renders base shapes, materials, patterns, symbols, ornaments and effects.
- Copper, Silver and Gold have dedicated prerendered border textures.
- The shield laboratory currently exposes 31 proposals, of which 17 are V1 candidates.
- `PlayerCardView` and Team Shield materials already share a small material vocabulary.
- Premium Ball remains `READY_PENDING_PHYSICAL_QA`; interactive GLB/sensor behavior is excluded.

## Pack targets

The V1 pack will select approximately 24 clearly differentiated proposals across:

- abstract football, tower, star, bolt, crown, monogram, nested badge, Future IQ and Barrio symbols;
- Barrio, Elite, Future IQ and Retro crowns;
- single, triple, premium and patch stars;
- improved laurels and banner alternatives;
- Copper, Silver, Gold, Matte Black, Chrome, Carbon and Navy material studies;
- restrained edge glow and material highlights;
- shared player/team material primitives where they do not imply shared ownership.

## Decision contract

| Decision | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| MANTENER | Strong laboratory candidate for later catalog review |
| REVISAR | Direction is useful but needs visual or product refinement |
| DESCARTAR | Does not meet legibility, identity or performance requirements |

Each final row will record name, type, collection, 2D/3D/pseudo-3D technique, player/team reuse, asset size, LOD behavior, performance and decision.

## Protected boundaries

- No production catalog migration.
- No ownership grant or new reward mapping.
- No Season, TOPS or tournament authority invented for future-looking art.
- No global WebGL, permanent animation or production sensor behavior.
